# FORM11 TECH STUDIOS — Portfolio handoff

## Case-study title
**VANTA DISTRICT — Building a Storefront for the Hours Between**

## Project summary
An independent streetwear storefront concept designed around the fictional VANTA DISTRICT label and its NIGHT SHIFT drop. The experience balances high-impact campaign art direction with a useful, low-friction shopping journey.

## Fictional client profile
- **Industry:** Contemporary streetwear / fashion retail
- **Audience:** Style-conscious people drawn to oversized silhouettes, limited drops and independent urban culture
- **Status:** Self-initiated fictional concept project. VANTA DISTRICT is not a real client and has not commissioned FORM11 TECH STUDIOS.

## Design rationale
Obsidian black and warm bone create an editorial foundation; acid orange acts as a deliberate signal for important actions and drop information. Space Grotesk gives headlines a confident, compressed rhythm, while DM Mono handles codes, labels and small operational details. Full-bleed photography, asymmetric editorial sections, precise borders and purposeful motion keep the site from feeling like a generic catalogue template.

## Implemented features
- Responsive editorial homepage and mobile navigation
- Eight fictional products across tops, outerwear and accessories
- Shop category filters and sorting by price and newest
- Individual product views with size, colour and quantity selection
- Persistent browser shopping bag, quantity updates, item removal and calculated subtotals
- Newsletter email validation with an explicit demo-only confirmation
- Demo checkout review state without payment or order submission
- Keyboard focus states, semantic form labels and reduced-motion support
- Relative paths for GitHub Pages project-site hosting
